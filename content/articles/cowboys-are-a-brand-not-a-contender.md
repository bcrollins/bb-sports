Stop.

Stop putting the Cowboys in your top 5. Stop putting them in your top 10. The Cowboys are a brand. They are not a team. **A brand cannot win you a playoff game in January.**

Twenty-eight years of no NFC Championship game appearances. Twenty-eight. I'm not editorializing — that's the count. Five different head coaches. Three different general manager stints. A litany of "the year it finally comes together" Augusts that turn into the same January where Dallas hosts a divisional, plays scared, gets out-coached, gets out-physicaled, and goes home for the offseason to do the same thing again.

The roster build is the worst part. The Cowboys keep treating the franchise like a museum: pay the legacy guys, refuse to draft offensive line in the first round, refuse to spend on a real second receiver, refuse to admit the defensive front needs a complete overhaul. **They are running it back with a roster that has not gotten past the second round of the playoffs in three decades.**

I'm done. I'm done with the analysts who put Dallas at #4 in the preseason because the helmet sells ad time. I'm done with the "Dak finally figured it out" takes that get recycled every September. I'm done with the "Jerry knows what he's doing" cope. **Jerry has not won a thing in this millennium.** That is the indictment.

Drop them. The roster does not warrant top-10. The brand warrants top-3 in hat sales and nothing else. Argue with me in the comments.

— **Brad**
