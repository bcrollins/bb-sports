> Disclosure: I'm a Florida Gators fan and a UF student. The reason this column is harder on Florida than the message board would like is that *I have to be honest with myself before I'm honest with anybody else.*

Florida football is **one quarterback away** from being in the SEC conversation again. The line is good enough. The skill is recruitable. The defense, on its best day, can hold a top-10 SEC offense to under thirty.

And I do not think we have the quarterback.

I've watched every snap this spring. The arm talent is real. The decision-making is not where it needs to be in the SEC. The play-action is where it needs to be in Group of Five. **Those are not the same level of football and I am tired of Florida fans pretending the gap closes itself in August.** It does not.

The job for the program in the next 18 months is honest with itself: either the staff develops the QB room into an SEC-grade starter and a real backup, or the portal gets used like Texas A&M used it in their best year — aggressively, expensively, with no romance about the recruit who was supposed to be the savior. **Pick a lane, and pick it before September.**

I am not going to write a Gators column that says everything is fine. Everything is not fine. The program is closer than it was three years ago. It is not as close as the orange and blue brigade keeps saying. **One quarterback away. We do not have the quarterback. Yet.**

Disclosure renewed at the bottom: still a fan, still going to every home game, still texting the group chat at 2 a.m. about the offensive line. **The fan view doesn't mean lying to the fan.**

— **Brad**
