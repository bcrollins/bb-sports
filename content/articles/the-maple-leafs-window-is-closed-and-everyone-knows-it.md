I am tired of writing the same Toronto Maple Leafs column every May.

**Eleven years.** One — *one* — playoff series win. Same core. Same first-round exits. Same heroic regular seasons followed by April collapses that the analysts keep calling "shocking" like they haven't watched the franchise's last decade unfold in front of them on the same calendar week every year.

The "give them more time" argument is dead. The "this is the year the experience kicks in" argument is dead. The "Matthews finally has the supporting cast" argument is dead. The core they built around — and paid a king's ransom to keep together — has produced *exactly one* playoff series win across their entire prime. **You don't get more time after eleven years.** You get a roster teardown.

The cap structure is the real indictment. Four max-money forwards. Goalie carousel. Defensive corps that's a different group of bottom-pair guys every spring. The front office keeps acting like the next free-agent depth signing is the missing piece. **The missing piece is the four guys eating $40M of the cap.** That's the conversation Toronto refuses to have because the conversation is hard and the brand is convenient.

Drop them on the rankings. I had Toronto at #6 because the regular season ceiling is real. The playoff ceiling is the playoff floor and it has been for over a decade. The window did not close last week. It closed years ago. **Toronto is the only city that doesn't know it.**

Either blow it up or stop pretending. There is no third option.

— **Brad**
