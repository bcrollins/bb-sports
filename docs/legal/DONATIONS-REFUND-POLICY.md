# BB Sports Donations And Refund Policy

Updated: 2026-05-08

## Status

Reader support is a launch-readiness feature. Payment movement remains disabled until the BB Sports Stripe account and payment link are verified for public launch.

## Terms

- Reader support is voluntary.
- Articles remain free. BB Sports does not sell premium article access.
- Support does not buy coverage, placement, a favorable opinion, silence, or editorial influence.
- Sponsorships are separate commercial relationships and must be disclosed.
- BB Sports is not presenting reader support as a tax-deductible charitable contribution.

## Refunds

Accidental or duplicate donations may be refunded within 30 days when requested through the BB Sports contact form. Refunds return to the original payment method when Stripe supports it.

## Provider Boundary

Stripe is the only approved payment rail for donations. The first-party BB Sports database remains the supporter-interest ledger and donation-intent record. When Stripe Checkout is configured, BB Sports creates the Checkout Session server-side and reconciles signed Stripe webhooks before marking a donation paid.

## Public Surface

The public reader-facing version of these terms lives at `/support/terms`.
